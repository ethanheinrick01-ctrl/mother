## Applied authoring rules
- One discriminating decision per root. Use causal consequences, function localization, comparisons, sequence, and findings that change interpretation; names/ages/wording alone never create a root.
- Match category, grammar, specificity and reasonable detail across options. Short nouns and phrases are characteristic; no explanatory key standing out among terse distractors. Do not pad equal word counts.
- Stems commonly end with a question mark or an incomplete sentence and colon; options contain initial capitals and occasional parenthetical terms. Authored items use complete questions, plain parallel options and no uniquely decorated key. Explanations and source caveats follow checking.
- Use plausible neighboring concepts from the packet. Avoid an obviously irrelevant distractor merely because a released item used one.
- Hide concept names on the active question screen when they would identify the answer. Generic chapter/run headings only.
- Preserve all 12 source examples as identifiable self-checks; original mocks and practice cannot copy their tested association with cosmetic changes.
- All practice, Review, Boss and mock roots record this revision. Review the complete bank for answer support and distinct decisions in a separate pass by the same author; do not call that independent review.
