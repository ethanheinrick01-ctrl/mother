## Content and assessment inventory

Every chapter has six original topic-practice MC roots, six original mock MC roots (two per form), one original prose synthesis case, source-linked teaching and one Clear-narrated mechanism film. Total: 120 MC, ten ungraded cases, 71 rendered teaching cards across the chapters, and 30 mastery-eligible concepts. Shared guide prompts can produce a card in more than one chapter. The 51 prompt-level counts are not the rendered-card count.

Each eligible concept has at least two distinct practice decisions. Questions were reviewed semantically, not counted as distinct merely because a name, age, or phrasing differs. All three mocks contain different item identities and decisions. Boss references selected practice roots; it is not falsely counted as 25 additional originals.

Prose is deliberately ungraded: drafts, model comparisons and self-ratings persist, but do not award mastery. Released examples remain in the private intake and are catalogued by sample ID in professordna.md. None is presented as a newly authored mock.

Mastery requires two consecutive unassisted correct answers on distinct roots with the latest confidence medium or high. Reading, watching, repeated exposure to one root, hints and prose self-ratings do not substitute for this evidence. New concepts must receive new IDs and new evidence.
