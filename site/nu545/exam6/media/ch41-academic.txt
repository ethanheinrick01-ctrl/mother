Intestinal contents normally move along the lumen. Here, the moving contraction advances material through an open segment.

A mechanical obstruction places a lesion in that route. Incoming contents collect upstream. Watch the proximal segment expand while material fails to pass the narrowed region. Chapter forty-one explains that a lower small-bowel obstruction can produce more distention because a greater length of bowel lies upstream.

Paralytic ileus is different. The packet describes failure of motility, especially after surgery, without an obstructing lesion. The lumen remains open, but the propulsive wave stops. The animation compares these mechanisms schematically; its dimensions do not represent a patient or a clinical threshold.
