## Narration and editable source

Six original scripts were checked against the exact course slides cited in each film. AI Voice Generator, Clear, supplied all completed narration. The signed provider download URLs and private projects are excluded. No subscription purchase or upgrade was made. The shared account showed 30K characters/month and sufficient existing quota; observed usage rose from 3K through 8K to 14K while concurrent exam work also used the account. These figures are observations, not itemized cost attribution. Playback is bundled locally and costs $0 per use.

The animation-source folder contains scripts, actual word/stage timings, caption alignment, the Pillow/FFmpeg renderer and manifest builder. Local Whisper timing is an alignment aid; captions retain the written script rather than replacing medical terms with recognition output. Narration and schematic overlays do not award mastery.
