Chapter five. Obesity is a risk factor for type two diabetes, but the connection is more than a label. The supplied notes describe insulin resistance: cells have difficulty using the insulin present.

Watch the tissue response weaken while insulin remains available. Contrast this with type one diabetes, where autoimmune destruction reduces the pancreatic beta-cell supply.

Family history also changes risk. Exercise and weight management address modifiable contributors; they do not erase inherited susceptibility.
