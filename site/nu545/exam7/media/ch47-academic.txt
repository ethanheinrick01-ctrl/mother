Atopic dermatitis involves epidermal barrier dysfunction and immune dysregulation. Filaggrin alteration is one supplied barrier mechanism.

Severe itching drives scratching. Watch the surface become disrupted, followed by a thickened epidermis with repeated scratching. The thickening is lichenification.

Hydration and emollients support barrier care. Distribution changes with age: infants often have face and extensor lesions, while older children favor flexures.
