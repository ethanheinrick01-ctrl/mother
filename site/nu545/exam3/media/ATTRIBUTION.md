Films crop and rescale the source art, animate tissue states, and add schematic hormone, fluid, cell, microorganism and inflammatory layers. Timing, scale, quantities and overlay colors are teaching conventions. The white-adipose histology is not relabeled as brown tissue; the brown-tissue comparison is explicitly schematic. The CDC image depicts cultured organisms on rabbit cell culture, not a human lesion. In situ versus invasive cancer is a comparison, not inevitable progression. Syphilis stage changes are not a prediction for an individual patient.

The built-in image-generation tool removed annotations from three animation backgrounds: the renal aquaporin, testis, and lymphatic illustrations. Each was visually compared with its original before animation. These layers are AI-assisted adaptations of credited academic art; the original reference images remain available. Details are in `animation-source/IMAGE-CLEANUP-PROVENANCE.md`.
