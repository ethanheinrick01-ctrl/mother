Two different failures can raise blood glucose. Start with the pancreatic beta cells. In type one diabetes, beta cells are destroyed or lost, and insulin production falls. Watch the declining insulin supply.

In type two diabetes, insulin resistance and decreased beta-cell secretion are the major mechanisms. The liver, muscle, and adipose tissue respond less effectively to insulin. Seeing insulin present does not prove that its effect is adequate.

Blood glucose can remain elevated in both comparisons. The common finding is hyperglycemia. The question is whether the problem is secretion, tissue response, or both.

Keep diabetes mellitus separate from diabetes insipidus. Mellitus concerns insulin and glucose. The insipidus lesson concerns antidiuretic hormone and urine concentration.
