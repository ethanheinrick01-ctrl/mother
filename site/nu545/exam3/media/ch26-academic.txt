Locate the testis, epididymis, and vas deferens on the anatomical illustration. The testis produces sperm. Sperm mature in the epididymis, and the vas deferens transports them toward the urethra.

For epididymitis, reverse that usual direction. The supplied slide describes a microorganism ascending the vas deferens from an infected bladder or urethra to reach the epididymis. Watch the highlighted inflammatory change develop at the epididymis.

Epididymitis is inflammation of the epididymis. Orchitis is infection of the testis and may occur with epididymitis. Similar pain does not make these the same affected structure.

The epididymitis slide lists pain and the Prehn sign, with antibiotics, analgesics, ice, and scrotal elevation as treatments. It does not provide a complete organism or complication list. Those textbook details remain marked as gaps in this guide.
