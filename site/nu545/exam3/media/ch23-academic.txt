Adipose tissue is active tissue. White adipose stores excess energy as triglycerides and acts as an endocrine organ. Its adipokines participate in energy balance, insulin sensitivity, and inflammatory responses. Watch the stored fat expand within the illustrated cells.

Brown adipose is rich in mitochondria. Cold exposure and sympathetic activation can stimulate it to generate heat through uncoupling protein one. Beige tissue can emerge within white adipose with chronic cold exposure or exercise. With warm adaptation, beige tissue can return to white adipose.

In obesity, macrophages infiltrate adipose tissue and inflammatory mediators are released. The supplied slides connect these changes with possible insulin resistance. More stored fat, altered endocrine signals, and inflammation belong to the same mechanism. Fat storage and heat production are different functions. Keep white, brown, and beige adipose distinct.
